Tool spotlight

Three ways AI works without you asking

Connectors

Claude reads from your accounts, like Gmail, so you stop copying and pasting.

Example: reply to an inbox lead

Scheduled tasks

A saved job runs at a set time and leaves the draft waiting.

Example: Monday market report

Cowork

Claude does a multi-step job with the files on your computer, right now.

Example: sort listing photos
