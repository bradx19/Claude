Q&A

Questions people wish they’d asked

Q

Can it read all my email?

It reads what you ask it to, when you ask. You decide what gets sent.

Q

Will clients know I used AI?

Your two skills keep it in your voice. You still read it before it goes out.

Q

What if it makes something up?

Ask it to flag anything it guessed. Check every fact before you post.

Q

What should I never paste in?

Social Security numbers, bank or loan details, anything a client asked to keep private.

Q

Does it remember what I told it?

Inside a Project, yes. A brand-new chat starts blank.

Q

Can I change my voice later?

Anytime. Edit the skill, or rebuild it from newer emails.
