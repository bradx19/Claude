Session 1 · How AI works

This is the live part. Step 1: show where connectors live and connect Gmail. This doubles as their tour of connectors. Step 2: ask Claude to read the last 20 relevant emails they sent to clients and pull out how they write: words they use, tone, length, how they open and close. Step 3: paste in links to their website and social profiles so it sees their public voice too. Step 4: read the draft voice out loud and ask "Does this sound like us?" Edit it until they say yes, then save it as their brand voice skill. Remind them it only reads what they point it at.
