Stock prompts

Four phrases that fix what AI gets wrong

It guesses

“Ask me questions before you start.”

It sounds flat

“Make it engaging and easy to read.”

It leaves things out

“Do a missed opportunities check.”

It contradicts itself

“Run a continuity and consistency check.”

Don’t hobble it

A limit becomes a target

“Keep it under 80 words.”

“As short as it can be without losing anything.”

Session 1 · How AI works · All of these are on your cheat sheet

These are the stock prompts I use every day. Each one fixes a specific frustration, so teach it as problem, then phrase: when it guesses, ask it to ask you questions; when it sounds flat, ask for engaging and easy to read; when it leaves things out, run a missed opportunities check; when names, numbers or tone drift, run a continuity and consistency check. Then the trap: hobbling. Any hard limit you give it becomes a target. Say "under 80 words" and a reply that needed 30 words comes back padded to 80. Describe what good looks like instead of capping it. Hand out the cheat sheet here; the rest of the phrases are on it.
