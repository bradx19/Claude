Session 1 · How AI works

Three things turn generic into you. Voice: how your team talks; we build the brand voice skill today. Context: the reference material you hand it, like the listing sheet, the client's email, or past posts you liked. AI can only use what it can see. Outcome: what you want to happen after someone reads it, like a booked showing or a reply. The bottom strip shows all three in one short prompt, so they can copy the pattern tomorrow.
