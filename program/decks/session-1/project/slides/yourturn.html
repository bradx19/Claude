Cheat sheet

Watch, then try. Demo it first, the same way we built the Big Bear post in the intro meeting. Step 1: show where their skills live (Customize, then Skills) and that all three are on: the brand voice skill they just built, the anti-AI skill, and the listing carousel skill. The last two come from the download links on the next slide, or from the team account if Jared provisions them org-wide before the session. Step 2: paste a listing link or a few photos plus the MLS text. That's the context. Step 3: ask for the post. That's the outcome. Step 4: improve it with one stock prompt, like "Do a missed opportunities check." Then each person does it with their own listing while you guide.
