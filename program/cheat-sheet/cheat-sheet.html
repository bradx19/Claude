Team AI Program · Keep this by your desk

Say this to get better results

Copy these phrases into any prompt. They work in Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI tool.
Ask first→Draft→Check it→Refine
1
Start right
before it writes
Ask me questions before you start.
It finds what's missing instead of guessing.
This is for [who]. They care about [what].
It writes to your reader, not to everyone.
Here's an example I like. Match it.
One example beats a paragraph of description.
Use my voice file.
Sounds like your team, not a robot.
2
Shape it
while it writes
Give me 3 versions: short, warm, and bold.
Pick the best one instead of settling.
Make it as short as it can be without losing anything.
A hard limit becomes a target, so it pads to fill it.
Make it engaging and easy to read.
Short lines, plain words, a clear next step.
Write it like a text, not an email.
Sets the tone in six words.
3
Check it
before you send
Do a missed opportunities check.
What did I leave out that would help?
Run a continuity and consistency check.
Names, numbers, and tone match all the way through.
Flag anything you guessed.
Separates real facts from filler.
Do a fair housing check.
Describe the home, never who should live there.
4
Refine it
when it's close
Keep everything else. Only change [this].
Stops it from rewriting what already works.
Rate it 1 to 10, then make it a 10.
It critiques itself, then fixes the gaps.
Read it as a skeptical buyer. What's weak?
Finds the holes before a client does.
Save this as a template I can reuse.
Do the work once, use it every week.
Stack them

"Ask me questions before you start. Make it engaging and easy to read. Then do a missed opportunities check."

Never paste in

Social Security numbers, bank or loan details, or anything a client asked you to keep private.
