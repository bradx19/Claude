Questions

Good questions.

Don't see yours? Ask me on the free call, or send me an email.

I'm not a tech person. Is this for me?

Yes. Most of my clients aren't. If you can send a text, you can do this. I explain everything in plain language and we go at your pace.

Which AI tools will I use?

We use the tools that fit your work, like ChatGPT and Claude. On our first call I'll tell you what's worth paying for and what the free versions can already do.

Is my client information safe?

We set clear rules from day one: what never goes into an AI tool, how to keep client details private, and how to review everything before it goes out.

Will it sound like me or like a robot?

Like you. A big part of what we do is teaching AI your voice, so drafts come back sounding like something you'd actually write.

What happens on the free call?

We talk about your week, I show you one thing on your own work, and I give you an honest recommendation. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you, and point you to a free place to start.
